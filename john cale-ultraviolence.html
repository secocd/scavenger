my notes:

All my life John Cale has attraced me in some way but never really got to like his music that much. Especially his album Fear. That changed recently when I listened to Amsterdam from this album. I got obsessed by it and listened to it in loop for a while. With time i got to enjoy his other albums like Songs for Dreila with Lou Reed and Wrong Way Up with Brian Eno more. I feel like John Cale is one of those artist which i had to force myself into liking because i knew that it would be worth it. And it was. I also really like his album covers, especially this one.

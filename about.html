about scavenger

Scavenger is a catalogue of CDs I find in charity shops, second-hand stores, and flea markets. The aim is to spend €3 or less per CD.

This isn't my entire music collection. I actually own 1000+ CDs that I've found in the wild.

I made this website to keep track of what I find, where I found it, how much I paid for it, and whatever memories end up being attached to it. I'm doing all of this manually, so it will take some time for me to upload all the CDs in my collection.

Started in 2022.
